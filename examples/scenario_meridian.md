# Meridian Health Data — FY27 Product Strategy

*Fictional. Written as a VP of Product would actually write it: OKR-shaped, honest,
and not a strategy. Used by `examples/session_walkthrough.md`.*

**Author:** Priya Raman, VP Product
**Audience:** CPO, then exec review

## Objective

Become the platform of record for care-quality reporting in post-acute care.

## Key Results

- KR1: Grow ARR from $18M to $30M by end of FY27
- KR2: Improve logo retention from 84% to 92%
- KR3: Ship the analytics rebuild by Q3 FY27
- KR4: Reach 40 NPS

## Where we compete

We sell to post-acute providers — home health, hospice, skilled nursing. Any
provider with regulatory reporting obligations is a fit. TAM is roughly $2.4B.
We segment as enterprise (>50 locations), mid-market, and small.

## Why we win

We are the most trusted name in the space, with the deepest regulatory content
library. Customers should recognise that our compliance coverage is more
complete than competitors', and as they do, we expect to win on renewal. Our
moat is fifteen years of regulatory content that would be very hard to rebuild.

## Roadmap themes

1. Analytics rebuild (Q1–Q3)
2. Expand regulatory content to two new state programmes
3. Improve onboarding time-to-value
4. Launch partner API
5. Invest in AI-assisted chart review

## Capabilities

Regulatory expertise. Data engineering. Customer success.

## Measures

ARR, logo retention, NPS. Reviewed at quarterly business review.

## Notes

Pricing is owned by the commercial team. Packaging changes need CRO sign-off.
The analytics rebuild is already committed to three enterprise customers.
