# Northwind Logistics — Growth Strategy FY27

*A fictional example, written deliberately badly, for exercising the assessment
path. Every failure mode in it is one the graph is built to catch.*

## Our Vision

To be the leading, most trusted, customer-centric logistics partner in North
America, known for world-class service and operational excellence.

## Our Goals

- Grow revenue by 18% CAGR through FY27
- Reach $450M in revenue and 12% EBITDA margin
- Improve NPS from 31 to 50

## Our Market

We serve all shippers who need reliable freight management. Our total
addressable market is $84B. We segment our customers as enterprise, mid-market
and SMB, and we go to market through third-party freight brokers as well as
direct.

## Strategic Priorities

1. Launch the new customer portal by end of FY26
2. Invest in digital and data capabilities
3. Expand into the Southeast region
4. Improve service levels across all accounts
5. Build a high-performing talent pipeline
6. Optimize network costs

## How We Win

We offer the best service quality at the most competitive price in the market.
Customers should recognise the value of our reliability, and as they do, we
expect to take share from incumbents. Our moat is our 40-year reputation, which
is unassailable.

## Capabilities

Digital. Data. Talent. Customer focus.

## Measures

Revenue, EBITDA margin, market share. Reviewed annually at the FY27 planning
offsite.

## Plan

Phase 1 (Q1 FY26): portal build. Phase 2 (Q3 FY26): Southeast launch.
Workstream owners to be assigned. Funded from operating efficiencies.
